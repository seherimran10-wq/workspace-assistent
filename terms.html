Workspace Assistant ("the App") is a portfolio demonstration built by Seher Studio to showcase an AI agentic workflow pattern (reasoning, confirm-before-act, and action) applied to Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive. It is provided as-is, free of charge, for evaluation purposes.

2. Your Google Account connection

Connecting your Google Account is optional. Without it, the App runs entirely on built-in sample data. If you connect, you are granting the App the specific permissions shown on Google's consent screen, and you can revoke that access at any time from your Google Account settings.

3. Confirm-before-act

Any action that would change your Google data (sending an email, creating a calendar event) is shown to you in a confirmation step before it runs, except when you have explicitly switched "Auto-Reply Mode" to Active, in which case Gmail replies are sent immediately without a further prompt.
