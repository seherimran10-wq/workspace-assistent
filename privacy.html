Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Workspace Assistant ("the App") is a private application built by Seher Studio that lets an AI agent read, draft, and act on a user's Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive with their explicit permission and a confirm-before-act safeguard.

What data we access

When you connect your Google Account, the App requests the following scopes, only after you explicitly grant consent through Google's own sign-in screen:

  • Gmail (read): to display a short list of your recent inbox messages (sender, subject, snippet) inside the App.
  • Gmail (send): to send a reply you have explicitly reviewed and confirmed inside the App.
  • Calendar (events): to display upcoming events and create new events you have explicitly reviewed and confirmed inside the App.
  • Drive (read): to search and display files matching a query you type inside the App.

How we use your data

All data fetched from your Google Account is used solely to render it inside the App's interface in your own browser session, and to carry out the specific action you confirm (sending a reply, booking an event, opening a file). We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the feature you directly triggered.

Where your data is stored

The App is a client-side web application. Your Google access token and any data fetched from your account are held only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session. Apart from the AI assistant requests described below, nothing is sent to, or stored on, any server operated by Seher Studio. Closing the tab or signing out clears this data immediately. We do not maintain a database of user data.

AI assistant

When you are connected to Google and type a request in the chat box, the App sends your request and your Google access token to a small Seher Studio server function. That function first checks the token is genuine, then passes your request to Anthropic's Claude API. To answer, Claude can look things up in your own account through that function: it can search and read your Gmail messages, list your upcoming Calendar events, and search your Drive file names and contents. Only what Claude needs for your request is looked up, and the text it reads is sent to Anthropic to produce the answer. The function does not store or log your emails, calendar or files, and Anthropic processes them only to produce your response. Claude can only read and propose: any email it writes is shown to you as an editable draft, and any calendar event as a confirmation, and nothing is sent or created until you approve it. Your data is used only to answer your request; it is not used for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

If the AI assistant is unavailable, the App falls back to a built-in mode that builds drafts inside your browser from the same information, without sending it anywhere.

Social Posting composer

The Social Posting module lets you write a caption and pick an image or video to prepare a post for Instagram, Facebook or TikTok. The file you choose is previewed inside your browser only and is never uploaded by the App. Your caption draft and platform selection are saved in your browser's local storage on your device so they survive a refresh; the media file is not saved. The App does not connect to Instagram, Facebook or TikTok and does not post on your behalf; the "Copy caption & open" buttons only copy your caption to your clipboard and open that platform's own website in a new tab.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your Google Account data. The only third-party service that receives any of it is Anthropic's Claude API, and only to answer the requests you make to the AI assistant described above. Google's own API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, governs our use of data obtained through Google APIs.

Revoking access

You can revoke Workspace Assistant's access to your Google Account at any time from your Google Account security settings.
