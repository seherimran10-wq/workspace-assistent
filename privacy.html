Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Workspace Assistant ("the App") is a private application built by Seher Studio that lets an AI agent read, draft, and act on a user's Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Drive with their explicit permission and a confirm-before-act safeguard.

What data we access

When you connect your Google Account, the App requests the following scopes, only after you explicitly grant consent through Google's own sign-in screen:

  • Gmail (read): to display a short list of your recent inbox messages (sender, subject, snippet) inside the App.
  • Gmail (send): to send a reply you have explicitly reviewed and confirmed inside the App.
  • Calendar (events): to display upcoming events and create new events you have explicitly reviewed and confirmed inside the App.
  • Drive (read): to search and display files matching a query you type inside the App.

How we use your data

All data fetched from your Google Account is used solely to render it inside the App's interface in your own browser session, and to carry out the specific action you confirm (sending a reply, booking an event, opening a file). We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than the feature you directly triggered.

Where your data is stored

The App is a client-side web application. Your Google access token and any data fetched from your account are held only in your browser's memory for the duration of your session. Apart from the AI email drafting described below, nothing is sent to, or stored on, any server operated by Seher Studio. Closing the tab or signing out clears this data immediately. We do not maintain a database of user data.

AI email drafting

When you ask the chat box to send an email, the App reads, in your browser, your recent emails with that recipient and your calendar for the next 10 days, and sends that information, together with your request, to a small Seher Studio server function. That function passes it to Google's Gemini API to write a draft for you to review. The function does not store or log any of it, and Google's Gemini API processes it only to produce that draft, under Google's terms for that API. Nothing is sent until you press Send on the draft. This data is used only to write your draft; it is not used for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your Google Account data. The only third-party service that receives any of it is Google's Gemini API, and only for the AI email drafting described above. Google's own API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, governs our use of data obtained through Google APIs.

Revoking access

You can revoke Workspace Assistant's access to your Google Account at any time from your Google Account security settings.
